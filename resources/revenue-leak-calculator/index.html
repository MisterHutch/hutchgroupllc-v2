How to interpret the numbers

Illustrative formula: monthly inquiries × assumed not-followed-up fraction × hypothetical booking probability × typical job value.

Example: 60 inquiries × 20% follow-up gap × 30% possible bookings × $1,500 = $5,400 scenario value. The largest source of uncertainty is whether the ignored inquiries were qualified and likely to buy. Use this to frame discovery, not budget a return.
