Before: it depends on memory

  1. A new inquiry lands in a shared inbox.
  2. Someone copies details to a spreadsheet.
  3. The team assumes someone else replied.
  4. The next step is hard to find.

After: ownership is visible

  1. The inquiry enters one shared record.
  2. A named owner receives a follow-up task.
  3. An acknowledgment explains what happens next.
  4. The team tracks the reply and next appointment.

This is a proposed workflow, not a measured customer outcome. For a real engagement, we would compare response time, follow-up completion, and booked conversations before and after the change.

A focused first engagement

Know what happens next.

The free conversation identifies the handoff to investigate. A paid audit or build starts only after you agree the scope and price.

Do we need to replace our CRM or website?

Not necessarily. We first review the tools and workflow you already use. The fix may be a clearer owner, a better form, or a small integration.

What should I bring to the review?

One example of how an inquiry reaches your team and what happens next. Please keep customer personal information and account credentials out of the request form.

What can the work include?

A shared intake record, ownership and routing rules, acknowledgment messages, reminders, scheduling handoffs, and reporting. We agree which pieces are justified before building.

What results will we measure?

Response time, the share of inquiries with an owner and next action, booked conversations, and paid engagements. We establish a baseline; we do not promise a conversion increase without evidence.

Do you work with businesses outside Minneapolis?

Our local focus includes Saint Paul and the Twin Cities metropolitan area. Tell us where your business operates in the review conversation.
