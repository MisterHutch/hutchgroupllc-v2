Common questions

No tech theater.
Just practical answers.

If your business runs on Google Workspace, Microsoft, an existing CRM, or even a couple of shared inboxes, we start from reality.

Do I have to buy AI software?

No. We choose tools only when they solve the problem. A better handoff or reminder may be enough.

Do you replace our CRM or website?

Not by default. We try to improve the workflow around your existing systems before recommending replacement.

Will this automatically increase my leads or sales?

No such result can be guaranteed. We help make your process observable, choose a meaningful baseline, and measure what changes after implementation.

Is this for businesses outside Minneapolis?

Yes. Hutchgroup is based in the Twin Cities and can work with service businesses throughout the metropolitan area and remotely when appropriate.
