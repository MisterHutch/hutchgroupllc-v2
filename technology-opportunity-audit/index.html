What we look for

Most businesses already know where it hurts. We help determine why.

Customer friction

Missed leads, slow response, confusing intake, weak follow-up, or a website that does not convert.

Operational friction

Duplicate entry, spreadsheet handoffs, manual reporting, recurring admin, or work that depends on one person remembering every step.

Technology friction

Disconnected systems, overlapping subscriptions, poor data flow, missing integrations, or tools that do not fit the process.

Decision friction

No clear roadmap, unclear ownership, vendor confusion, or too many ideas competing for limited time and budget.
