What this solves

When everybody owns technology, nobody really owns technology.

The owner chooses one platform, operations adds another, marketing buys a third, and someone eventually builds a spreadsheet to connect the gaps. Hutchgroup creates a coherent view of the business and keeps the roadmap tied to actual priorities.
