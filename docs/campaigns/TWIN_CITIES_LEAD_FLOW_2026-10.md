## Executive brief
**Audience:** Owner/operators and office managers of Minneapolis–Saint Paul and Greater Twin Cities small service businesses (initial test: home services, specialty repair/auto, professional services, flexible workplaces). Start with 2–30 employee firms as an internal prospecting hypothesis; paid-platform audiences may not support employee-count filters.

**The problem:** Inquiries enter through website forms, calls, email and social. Follow-up often depends on a person noticing a message. Buyers do not want another platform; they want a clear response and an owner for the next step.

**Lead promise:** **More inquiries. Less falling through the cracks.**  
**Support:** Get found. Follow through. Grow.  
**Brand philosophy:** Start with the problem. Not the technology.  
**Primary CTA:** **Free 15-minute Technology Opportunity Review**; this requests a conversation, not a confirmed appointment.  
**Secondary CTA:** Explore an actual functioning **synthetic** Hutchgroup Ops HVAC demonstration (not customer outcomes or real integrations).

### Pilot framing
**Hutchgroup Lead Flow Starter — proposed $1,500 starting price**, not a fixed comprehensive bundle or currently verified competitive market rate. Specific baseline scope: one inquiry path, one owner/notification rule, one follow-up/reminder behavior, basic testing and handover. Additional ads, subscription fees, SMS usage, integrations and ongoing services are scoped separately. Do not promise guaranteed lead growth, bookings, recovered revenue or specific AI features.

**Conversion ladder:** awareness/paid ad → Lead Flow Starter landing page → optional Ops sample → free review request → qualified discovery → priced scoped pilot → documented baseline and potential ongoing support. Existing `/digital-menu/` is available as an upsell/exploration destination, not the first ad click.

### Three budget choices (illustrative tests, not promised results)
- **$0 test:** organic Meta, LinkedIn, X, GBP; outreach only to appropriate warm/referral contacts. No paid ads.
- **$300 test:** Meta $10/day for 30 days, no paid Google or X; one campaign, two creative variations, same local landing page.
- **$600 test (recommended ceiling for first month, subject to Hutch's explicit approval):** Meta **$360** (~$12/day × 30 days) and Google Search **$240** (~$8/day × 30 days) only if Google produces enough relevant impressions at that budget. If Google click volume is too low, run **15 days at $16/day** rather than spreading too thin; never exceed $600 combined.

These are **spend caps for planning**, not vendor charges already committed. Platform billing can fluctuate around daily budgets; use account-level limits where supported and verify total spending. Pause and review before committing to a second month.

## Geographic targeting
**Core:** Minneapolis and Saint Paul, then nearby suburbs including Bloomington, Edina, Richfield, St. Louis Park, Minnetonka, Plymouth, Golden Valley, Maple Grove, Brooklyn Park, Roseville, Woodbury, Eagan, Burnsville and Blaine. Start at roughly 25–30 miles around Minneapolis or equivalent city inclusions and inspect platform map/forecast. Expand only after valid leads arrive outside the core.

**Google Search:** choose **Presence** (people in or regularly in the targeted area), not broad Presence or Interest, to reduce out-of-area clicks. See https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/1722038.

**Meta:** use available broad geographic placement; avoid asserting unavailable granular owner-job-title filters. Ad creative and destination should self-qualify owner/operators. Do not target protected or sensitive characteristics. Site targeting and ad delivery availability must be confirmed in the live account.

- Use only allowlisted UTMs in `assets/journey.js`.

- KPI hierarchy: **qualified review conversations → proposals → paid customers → review submissions → landing page visits**. CPC/CTR/impressions are diagnostic, not success on their own.

- Keep an operating sheet with date, channel, spent, impressions, clicks, landing visits, real form submissions, qualified reviews, appointments, proposals, won revenue, CAC when measurable.
