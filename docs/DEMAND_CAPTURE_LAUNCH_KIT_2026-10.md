### Hypotheses to test
- Owners respond more readily to missed follow-up than “AI transformation.”
- Seeing a synthetic workflow improves trust and clarity.
- Existing system integrations are easier to sell than “replace your tools.”
- Human approval boundaries increase acceptance of AI messaging.

If no qualified conversations emerge after 20 genuine, personalized approaches, revisit audience, proof and offer. Do not increase paid spend until a qualified conversion path is demonstrated.
