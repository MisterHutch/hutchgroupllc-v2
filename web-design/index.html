Frequently asked questions

Small business web design questions

Do you redesign existing websites or only build new ones?

Both can make sense. A focused redesign may preserve useful content and systems, while a new build may be cleaner when the existing site is difficult to maintain or no longer reflects the business.

How many pages does a small business website need?

There is no universal number. The right structure depends on the services customers search for, the questions they need answered, and the proof available. A smaller, genuinely useful site is better than many thin pages.

Will a new website automatically rank on Google?

No. A well-built site creates a stronger technical and content foundation, but visibility also depends on competition, relevance, authority, local business information, reviews, links, and continued useful work.

Can the site work with local SEO and follow-up tools?

Yes. The website can be structured to support local search and connect inquiries to an appropriate email, scheduling, CRM, or follow-up workflow based on your needs.
